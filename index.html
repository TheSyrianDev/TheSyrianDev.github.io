About

I'm a student in my first year of secondary school, and I spend the rest of my time in software. Seven years in the field, and the thread through all of it is AI: reading how models actually work, testing where they fall over, and writing the plumbing that lets one run usefully on a normal machine instead of someone else's server.

I also do technical support, which is where most of what I know about software being used comes from — the difference between a feature that exists and a person who can get through it. That shows up in how I build: defaults explained, failures named, no telemetry quietly phoning home.

Most of what I make is open to read at github.com/TheSyrianDev, and the suite is published under LastSoft.
